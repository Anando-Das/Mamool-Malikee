Our Story

Heritage, craftsmanship, and the pursuit of olfactory perfection

The Beginning

"Every great fragrance tells a story. Ours began in Sylhet."

Mamool Malikee was born from a deep reverence for Arabian perfumery traditions. Founded in Sylhet, Bangladesh, our journey started with a simple belief: that the ancient art of oud distillation and bakhoor preparation deserved to be preserved, honored, and shared with discerning noses around the world.

What began as a small family operation — sourcing raw agarwood, hand-grinding incense resins, and carefully blending attar oils — has grown into a brand recognized for its uncompromising quality and authentic character.

Why Choose Mamool Malikee

The principles that guide everything we create

Pure Ingredients

We use only 100% natural agarwood, musk, amber, and floral extracts. No synthetics, no compromises.

Handcrafted

Every batch is prepared using traditional methods passed down through generations of master perfumers.

Sustainable

We source our agarwood from ethical, sustainable harvests that protect the forests for future generations.

Authentic

Each product is a genuine expression of Arabian perfumery heritage, crafted with respect and precision.
