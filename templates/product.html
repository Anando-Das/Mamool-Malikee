OUD Malikee represents the pinnacle of our craft. Each batch is carefully distilled using traditional methods passed down through generations of perfumers. The agarwood used in this attar is sustainably sourced from mature trees, ensuring both ethical practices and the highest quality oil.

Unlike synthetic fragrances, our oud oil develops uniquely on each wearer's skin, creating a signature scent that is truly personal. The 6ml bottle provides approximately 180 applications, making it an exceptional value for a fragrance of this caliber.

100% Pure Agarwood (Aquilaria malaccensis) Oil

Our OUD Malikee contains no alcohol, no synthetic additives, and no diluting agents. The oil is extracted through hydro-distillation of infected agarwood heartwood, a process that yields the most precious and aromatic compounds of the tree.
