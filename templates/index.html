The Art of Layering

Understanding Fragrance Notes

Every Mamool Malikee fragrance is composed like a symphony — with top, heart, and base notes that unfold over time, revealing new dimensions with every hour.

  • Top The first impression — light, fresh, evaporating within minutes
  • Heart The soul of the fragrance — emerging after 15 minutes, lasting hours
  • Base The foundation — deep, rich notes that linger on skin and fabric
